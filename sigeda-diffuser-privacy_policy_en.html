Sigeda Diffuser ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you register for an account, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, password (encrypted)
  • Profile Information: Display name, profile picture (optional)
  • Contact Information: Email address for account verification and communication

2.2 Device Information

When you use the Service, we automatically collect certain device information:

  • Device Identifiers: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Bluetooth Information: Connected diffuser device IDs, connection logs
  • Usage Data: Device usage patterns, settings preferences, usage history

2.3 Usage Information

We collect information about how you use the Service:

  • Device Control Data: Mist intensity settings, lighting preferences, timer configurations
  • Usage Statistics: Frequency of use, session duration, feature usage
  • Performance Data: App crash reports, error logs, performance metrics

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

Important: We do NOT collect:

  • Your precise location data (GPS)
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Calendar information
  • Photos or media files (unless explicitly uploaded by you)
  • Payment information (all payments are processed by third-party processors)

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and Maintain the Service: Enable device connection, control, and monitoring

  • Communicate with You: Send service updates, security alerts, and support messages

  • Comply with Legal Obligations: Meet regulatory and legal requirements

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Data Storage

Your information is stored on secure servers located in data centers. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information.

5.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: Data transmitted between the app and servers is encrypted using SSL/TLS
  • Password Security: Passwords are hashed and salted before storage
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls limit who can access your information
  • Regular Audits: We regularly review our information collection, storage, and processing practices

5.3 Bluetooth Security

For Bluetooth connections:

  • Device pairing uses industry-standard encryption
  • Connection data is not transmitted to external servers
  • Local storage is encrypted on your device
